04 — Structure

Life happens, and the eye changes with it

2022 – 2025, RoamWithPete

Somewhere between 2022 and 2025, life got busier and my eye got sharper — not necessarily in that order. I started noticing buildings the way I used to notice landscapes: the logic of a facade, the geometry of a stairwell, the conceptual idea behind a space before it was ever built. My composition shifted too, toward wide 16:9 frames that feel more like film stills than snapshots. I started posting less and choosing more — fewer photos, but each one carrying more intention.
