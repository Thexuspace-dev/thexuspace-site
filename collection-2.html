02 — Stillness

2020, and the old photos kept me sane

2020

The world went quiet and stayed quiet. Isolation makes you strange company for yourself, and I found myself going back through old travel photos — trips I hadn't thought about in years — just to remember what an open door felt like. Somewhere in that habit of looking back, photography stopped being a souvenir and became something closer to a practice. I started paying more attention: to light, to framing, to what a photo could hold onto when everything else felt paused.
