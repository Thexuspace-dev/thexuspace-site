01 Approach

Design as a response, not a decoration.

My practice focuses on proactively solving the next decade's challenges through environmental-centric design. I utilize immersive space creation to subtly foster user awareness and responsibility — specializing in adaptive reuse, sustainable methods, and historical renovation, and refining my methodology using AI as a critical design-thinking tool.

This portfolio showcases work spanning a residential project for Restoration Hardware, a graduate thesis on floating architecture and interiors, commercial and hospitality studio designs, and furniture explorations that inform the interdisciplinary practice behind it all.

La Belle Élégance

229 Arch St, Philadelphia — Heritage & Hospitality

An adaptive reuse project transforming the historic Berger Building in Old City Philadelphia. The core objective is adaptive reinterpretation: merging the building's early-20th-century industrial heritage with the demands of luxury hospitality, anchored by a French-inspired restaurant and bar.

Material palette: dark walnut, Calacatta mint marble, white marble, antique brass.

Graduate Thesis

Iceberg Haven

Copenhagen, Denmark — Beyond the Waves

A sustainable, adaptive design proposal for Copenhagen's central canal: a social hub of floating hostel and central units engaging locals and tourists with the city's waterways while raising awareness of rising sea levels. The centerpiece "Iceberg Staircase" begins solid at the lower floors and gradually dissolves as users ascend — a physical metaphor for a melting iceberg.

Program includes a bi-level tavern, saunas, sundeck, underwater cinema, and an underwater farm.

03 Craft

Design proven by hand.

Furniture Studio

Escher's Cedar

A pure example of handmade craftsmanship, conceptually inspired by the continuous geometric illusion of the Penrose staircase. Constructed from solid cedar, the chair's structure uses traditional mortise and tenon and lap joints to achieve seamless structural connections — eliminating the need for visible hardware and letting the cedar's natural grain become the primary aesthetic feature.

30" high, 18" seat, 16.5" × 15" plan — fully hand-built, no fasteners.
