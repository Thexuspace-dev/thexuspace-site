Who is RoamWithPete?

I started this as Adventureaheadpx — just my dreams of traveling, caught in scraps of video. After-school sunsets, Sunday hikes, the little moments with friends I didn't want to forget.

Somewhere along the way it became more than that — a hobby, then a habit, then the thing that actually fuels how I see. And slowly, stills took over. I guess I figured: if one frame can tell you exactly how I felt in that moment, why hand you the whole video and give the trick away?

Now I just want to share it more honestly — something personal, so you can roam right alongside me.

Life happens, and the eye changes with it

Between 2022 and 2025, the way I looked at things changed. I got more observant — drawn into existing and conceptual architecture, the logic behind a facade or a stairwell. My composition shifted with it too, more horizontal, closer to 16:9 film framing, and I started posting the work differently to match — a shift I'm still refining today.
