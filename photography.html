RoamWithPete

Photography

A practice in composition and perspective that feeds directly back into the design work — started in 2019. Two images have since placed as AGORA Top 10 Finalists. Five chapters, so far — scroll to read them in order.

Scroll to walk through ↓
01Wander

Before it was a practice, it was just a family trip

It started with international travel — cameras out on family trips, memories for us, nothing more serious than that. In 2019, near Vancouver, I photographed a seal without thinking much of it, then submitted the shot on a whim. It placed as an AGORA Top 10 Finalist. I hadn't set out to become anything. I'd just liked the picture.

Vancouver, 2019

02Stillness

2020, and the old photos kept me sane

Then 2020 happened — the kind of year still strange to describe out loud. Isolation set in, and somehow it was the photos from before, from the freedom of past trips, that got me through it. Going back through them, then shooting again whenever I could, pulled me further into photography than any deliberate plan would have.

2020

03Wonder

Not just looking at stars — bathing in them

Then one night in Idaho. Not just looking up at stars, but standing under a sky so full it felt like bathing in a falling universe. Some photographs exist because you're documenting something. This one exists because I didn't want to forget what that felt like.

Idaho, 2021

04Structure

Life happens, and the eye changes with it

Between 2022 and 2025, the way I looked changed. I got more observant — drawn into existing and conceptual architecture. My composition shifted with it, more horizontal, closer to 16:9 film framing, and I started posting the work differently to match.

2022 – 2025, RoamWithPete

05Continuing

Today, still learning what my perspective is

And today — July 30, 2026 — this is still where I am: still looking, still figuring out what my own perspective is. Not a neat ending, just where the story happens to be standing right now. This isn't a closed chapter. It's the one I'm still writing.
