Who is RoamWithPete?

I started this as Adventureaheadpx — just scraps of video from after-school sunsets and Sunday hikes. It grew into a hobby, then the thing that fuels how I see.

Stills took over — one frame says more than a whole video ever could. Now I just want to share it honestly, so you can roam with me.

I'd tried to capture that mysterious dark sky before, but could never catch the one opportunity to take it all in — until this night in Idaho, far from anywhere, under a sky that felt endless. 2022 – 2025, RoamWithPete

Life happens, and the eye changes with it

Between 2022 and 2025, the way I looked at things changed. I got more observant — drawn into existing and conceptual architecture, the logic behind a facade or a stairwell. My composition shifted with it too, more horizontal, closer to 16:9 film framing, and I started posting the work differently to match — a shift I'm still refining today.
