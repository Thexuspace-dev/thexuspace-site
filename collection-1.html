Beginner's luck

Vancouver, 2019

It was just another day on my trip — the seal was lying there and got awakened by a nearby curious tourist. I was just able to grab a shot of him without thinking much of it, and it went on to place in the AGORA Top 10. In the end, I can't explain why — I just liked the picture.
