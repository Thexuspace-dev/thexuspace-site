01

Beginner's luck

Vancouver, 2019

This collection is a walk back to where it all started — blurry shots, over- or underexposed frames, the mistakes that come with not knowing yet. I was a curious kid teaching himself something new, one accident at a time. These photos aren't grouped by year; they're grouped by that feeling — the newness of it all, and not being able to wait to try every form of photography there was.
