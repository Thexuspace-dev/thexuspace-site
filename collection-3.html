03 — Bathed

My obsession with cold, dark nights in the middle of nowhere

Idaho, 2021

I'd tried to capture that mysterious dark sky before, but could never catch the one opportunity to take it all in — until this night in Idaho, far from anywhere, under a sky that felt endless. This shot exists because I didn't want to forget what that felt like, bathing in a universe that seemed to be falling all around me.
